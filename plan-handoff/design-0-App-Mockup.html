You may pursue a consumer grievance for the unresolved defect.

Based on the information provided, the repeated failure to restore a newly purchased refrigerator may support a complaint concerning a product defect or deficiency in service. Recommended next steps

1Send the seller and manufacturer a concise written grievance through an official channel, stating the defect and remedy requested.
2Keep proof of delivery and allow the documented response period under the applicable channel.
3If unresolved, consider the appropriate consumer commission or the official online filing channel.
